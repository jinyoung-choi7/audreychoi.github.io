02 · User Persona
The Optimized Backpacker

Meet Chris

Backpacks three times a year, three days to a week at a stretch. Gear-forward, sustainability-minded, allergic to inefficiency.

"Every small inefficiency adds up during a hike."

Needs
  • Gear for day and night, multi-day hiking
  • Easy hydration on longer trips
  • Visibility once the sun drops
  • A bottle he can actually keep clean
Behaviors
  • 3 trips a year, 3–7 days each
  • Values sustainability and durability
  • Expects one bottle to last six years
03 · Pain Points

Three tensions on the trail

01 Accessibility while moving

Hikers want hydration woven into the walk itself — no extra load, physical or mental.

02 Durability vs. sustainability

A growing pull toward materials that last for years and don't cost the planet anything.

03 Room for one more feature

Genuine openness to something new — especially a built-in light for visibility after dark.

04 · The Product

Introducing Bottleco Lumenis

A hands-free, illuminated hiking water bottle designed for easy access, visibility, and all-day durability on the trail.

Premium glass, 20–32 oz Wrapped in a protective silicone sleeve for grip and drop protection.
Built-in insulated tube A bite valve lets you sip without ever pulling the bottle from your pack.
Dial-controlled LED light Glows through the water itself, like carrying a lantern instead of a bottle.
Ergonomic, contoured grip Shaped for a secure hold on uneven terrain, day or night.
05 · Design Strategy

Who we're designing for

Who

Hikers, backpackers, campers — the outdoor-performance segment.

Needs

Reliability, weight, modularity, durability.

Key driver

Function over aesthetics — but never at the cost of either.
