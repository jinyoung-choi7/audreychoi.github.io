Research grounding

A population study on exercise motivation found that adherence to a fitness program depends heavily on how motivated a person feels day to day — and that motivation itself varies by exercise setting, gender, and relationship status.

That variability is the opening: a shared, game-based format gives people a reason to stay motivated that solo gym routines don't.

Source: Vučković & Đurić, population study on exercise motivation, PMC10991817

Mission statements, long & short

Short: To make fitness education more accessible and interactive through sensor-based technology that helps users safely learn and practice movement-based activities like yoga, Pilates, and Zumba in real time.

Long: Our mission is to make fitness education more accessible and engaging through sensor-based technology that provides real-time movement guidance for activities like yoga, Pilates, and Zumba. As a B-Corp, we use our for-profit model to improve access to interactive wellness tools, promote healthy lifestyles, and support long-term physical and mental well-being.

Impact is measured via user engagement (active minutes, sessions/week, retention), exercise consistency (repeat play), and access (lower price, accessibility feature usage).
