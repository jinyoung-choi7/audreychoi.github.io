Synthesis

Point of View

Vintage store owners in the East Village want to showcase their unique, ever-changing inventory and distinct styling in a clear, engaging way — because customers struggle to discover and visualize how items fit and look on a person, leading to lost sales and shopper frustration.

How Might We
  • Help vintage retailers visually communicate their curated styles and sizing accurately, without the hassle of traditional mannequins?
  • Enable customers to quickly and confidently browse and discover vintage pieces, both in-store and online?
  • Provide store owners with an easy, low-tech tool to create consistent styled looks that connect their offline and online shopping experiences?
06

Observations

East Village vintage shops excel in taste and styling, but struggle to showcase their unique offerings consistently and clearly to customers.

Current Challenges
  • Sizing uncertainty — shoppers can't easily tell how items fit
  • No searchable or browsable inventory catalogs
  • Styling relies on intuition, with no structured lookbooks
  • Mannequins are too expensive and there's no room in store
NXN Labs Solution
  • Visual browsing of styled looks before digging through racks
  • Sizing clarity with on-model previews
  • Virtual styling that eliminates manual dressing
  • Fast, no-technical setup for store owners

09

Executive Summary

Propose a pilot partnership between NXN Labs and East Village vintage stores to showcase the power of AI in fashion imagery — vintage stores gain cutting-edge content and inventory tools, NXN Labs gains a real-world use case in a culturally influential fashion vertical.

NXN Labs was built for fashion professionals generating high-quality model imagery, but it isn't yet tailored to everyday shoppers or vintage retailers. While its AI excels at styling and visual marketing, it lacks real-time inventory, sizing accuracy, and personalized try-ons. Vintage stores, dealing with unique pieces and fast turnover, need tools for quick uploads, streamlined inventory, and fit previews. NXN could expand its impact by evolving into a platform that combines its visual AI with practical retail tools — bridging high-end styling technology with real-world vintage retail needs.
